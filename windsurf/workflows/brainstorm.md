# /brainstorm — idea → validated approach

For ideas and open-ended asks with no committed approach. If the task is already
concrete, use /brief instead. Ask me ONE question per message, always as
suggested-responses multi-choice.

1. Ground in reality: check existing briefs, .agent/knowledge/ notes, and the
   relevant code area before proposing anything.
2. Refine the idea with single questions (purpose, who benefits, constraints,
   success criteria). Stop as soon as the shape is clear.
3. Optional: 1-2 Exa MCP searches — how do similar products/chatbots solve this?
   Cite findings as candidate approaches.
4. Present 2-3 approaches with trade-offs, leading with your recommendation and why.
   Apply YAGNI — strike anything not serving the success criteria. Let me pick via
   multi-choice.
5. Validate the chosen approach in small sections (~200 words: flow, components,
   error handling, rough effort), checking "look right?" after each via options.
6. Land the output:
   - Boss's call → a ready-to-send proposal message: "I see A or B, I lean A because…"
   - Mine to build → seed a /brief with the validated approach and save the
     exploration to .agent/briefs/NNN-<topic>-brainstorm.md.
Gate: no implementation and no /plan until the approach is validated (and approved
by my boss when it's his call).
